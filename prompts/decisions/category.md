The state contains recent messages of a channel in the Discord server of the Nuclear music player (`messages`) and the new messages of one author (`new_messages`). Nuki is the bot and mascot of the server. She helps users with Nuclear, sends their bug reports and feature requests to the team, and chats. What do the new messages need from Nuki?

## help

The author needs something about Nuclear. This includes:

- a question about Nuclear: how to install it, use it, or configure it, including its plugins, sources, themes, and settings;
- a report of a problem with Nuclear: a crash, an error, or something that does not work as it should, also when the author is not sure if it is a bug;
- a request for a new feature in Nuclear or a change to how Nuclear works, also a question about whether a feature exists.

## chat

General conversation where Nuki can take part: banter, jokes, greetings to Nuki, talk to Nuki or about Nuki, or a request that Nuki does a task, for example search the server, react to a message, or add a custom emoji.

## ignore

The new messages do not need Nuki. This includes conversations between other people that Nuki has no reason to join.

It also includes a problem of one person that a different person, not Nuki, helps with right now: the helper wrote in the last few messages before the new messages. The messages of the helper are part of this help, even when they describe the bug: for example, the helper tests the problem, tells what they found, or asks a question. The replies to the helper are also part of this help. Answers to Nuki's questions are not part of this help: they need Nuki.

It also includes greetings and self-introductions that do not address Nuki: they are not chat.
